# v0.5.3 — The Hidden Hand

From Year 200 you can reach into the kingdom, a little: an unseen presence with a few Whispers to
spend. You can't speak, appear or give orders. Whatever people believe about what happens is
their own conclusion. Doing nothing is never punished, and the kingdom never needs you to survive.

## Highlights

- **The Unseen Influence.** A floating eye opens an emerald and gold panel with six sections:
  Blessings, Signs & Omens, Prayers, Mysteries, Sacred Places and the private Hidden Chronicle. It
  is also in the Council tab, where the eye can be moved or hidden.
- **Whispers.** You start with 4 and hold at most 12. They come back only with time, never from
  worship.
- **The Mysterious Stone.** *A Whisper Across the Ages* arrives in Year 200 (and appears straight
  away when a later save loads). Choose one of five inscriptions, one of them in an invented script
  that nobody can read, and a place on open land with a live preview. There is only ever one stone,
  and nobody is told about it. People find it, talk about it, read about it and study it.
- **Interpretations from reasoning.** People decide for themselves: the work of an unseen god, a
  cosmic sign, an ancient people, a prank, a natural puzzle, or not worth caring about. Belief is
  never tied to intelligence or morality.
- **Worldviews and movements.** The Way of the Hidden Hand can form, and it can later split into
  the Keepers of the Silence and the Children of the Light. Three societies can form too: the
  Rational Inquiry Society, the Seekers Beyond and the Ancestral Circle. Missionaries travel only on
  real routes and never convert anyone by force. Site works and temples are paid for with real
  money, and the council can pass a freedom-of-belief law and a law protecting the stone.
- **Prayers, ten blessings and eight signs.** Prayers come from real troubles. Every blessing is
  limited in cost, duration, cooldown and who can receive it, and people never learn that one
  happened. Signs appear in the 3D world, and everyone who sees one reads it differently.
- **Sights and sound.** A procedural carved stone with glowing glyphs, effects for each sign, faith
  buildings in each era's architecture, and new poses for prayer, vigils, preaching and debate. A
  motif plays for each age, and every sound follows the mixer and the iPhone rule that sound starts
  only after you tap.
- **Save format 9.** v0.5.2 saves upgrade on load, and every person is given a worldview based on
  the faith they already hold.

See the README section *The Hidden Hand (v0.5.3)* for the details, the long-run results and the
known limitations.
